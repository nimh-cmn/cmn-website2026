The Data Science and Sharing Team supports investigators within the NIMH intramural program in creating, distributing, and leveraging large open datasets to accelerate discovery.

The team provides tools and training to help the IRP embrace open science and stay current with emerging datasets, platforms, and data-sharing practices.

- [Board of Scientific Counsellors report](/assets/DSST_BSC_Dec_2023_0.pdf)
- [Curated public dataset list](/dsst/datasets/)

NIMH investigators are invited to contact DSST to request updates, changes, or additions to datasets DSST is curating.
