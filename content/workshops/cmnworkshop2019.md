Short_description: The Center for Multimodal Neuroimaging hosted its first Multimodal Neuroimaging Workshop on October 30, October 31, and November 1, 2019. This three-day workshop at NIH brought together external speakers and internal NIMH scientists from diverse backgrounds in multimodal imaging methods and applications.
Description: The workshop was organized around four themes: lectures and demonstrations showcasing the value added by multimodal imaging; panel discussion of research and clinical questions that lend themselves to multimodal assessment; panel discussion of practical approaches for integrating multimodal imaging data; and discussion of the steps needed for future multimodal data collection, analysis, and interpretation.
